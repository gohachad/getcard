getcard is an independent intermediary. We are not a bank and we do not issue cards. We prepare the documents and file the application with a licensed Kyrgyz bank on your behalf, under a notarised power of attorney.

Apple Pay has worked since 28 July 2026

Apple Pay launched in Kyrgyzstan on 28 July 2026. The issuing bank was in the first wave, so the card adds to Wallet and works from day one.

Visa charges no extra fee for paying via Apple Pay. The fees and the exchange rate are the same as paying with the physical card.

Google Pay is supported as well.

What the card does

  • Payments and cash withdrawals abroad

    Pay in shops, cafés and terminals anywhere VISA is accepted. ATM withdrawals cost 1%, minimum $3.

  • Foreign subscriptions and online services

    Pay for foreign subscriptions and online services that no longer accept cards issued in Russia.

  • SWIFT transfers in dirhams

    Incoming and outgoing SWIFT transfers. In UAE dirhams (AED) only — transfers in dollars or euros are not available.

On notifying the tax authority

Opening an account with a foreign bank must be reported to the Russian tax authority within one month, and an annual statement of account activity is due after that. This is the account holder’s obligation rather than ours — but it is better to know now than in a year.

Questions

Do I have to travel to Kyrgyzstan?

No. Our representative files the application under a notarised power of attorney. You visit a notary in your own city once, and that is the only trip involved.

Why is a power of attorney needed?

The bank opens an account only in person — either the client or their representative. A notarised power of attorney lets our representative file the application on your behalf. It is limited to submitting the documents and opening the account: it does not allow anyone to move your money.

How long does it take?

From sending your documents to receiving the card: [TBD]. The exact time depends on how quickly you arrange the power of attorney.

Which documents are required?

An international passport, an internal passport, and a notarised power of attorney. Proof of income and proof of address are not required.

How do I add money to the card?

The account is in dollars and is topped up by transfer. Available methods: [TBD]. Topping up is an operation you carry out with the account directly; getcard is not involved in it.

Does the card work inside Russia?

No. It is a foreign bank’s card, and VISA and Mastercard have not processed transactions inside Russia since 2022. The card is for payments abroad and online.
